Old Fashioned

Circa 1880s • Pendennis Club / Louisville, KY

The Old Fashioned is the primordial cocktail: spirits, sugar, water, and bitters. Developed as bartenders began overcomplicating drinks with European cordials, purists demanded their libation made the "old-fashioned way."

Why Stir? Drinks consisting purely of spirits and sugars must be stirred with dense clear ice to achieve optimal chill (approx. 28°F) with silky, bubble-free dilution (around 20-25%). Shaking would over-aerate and cloud the spirit.
